About JAAD Logistics

Moving people forward.

Vision

Africa's most trusted logistics partner

A continent fully connected, where businesses move goods across borders without friction and Nigerian enterprise is represented at the highest global standard.

Mission

Delivering excellence, every mile

Reliable, efficient logistics solutions connecting Nigerian businesses to global markets — freight forwarding, international shipping, road haulage, RoRo transport, customs clearing and end-to-end supply chain management.

Behind every shipment is a business owner waiting for raw materials, a family expecting goods from abroad, or an entrepreneur expanding into new markets. We remove the friction — customs, multi-modal transport, time-sensitive cargo — so our clients can focus on what they do best.
